AIReels Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

AIReels Studio ("the app") is a desktop application that runs entirely on the user's own Windows computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube account, you grant the app access through Google OAuth to:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) — to publish videos you generated to your channel.
  • Read your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) — to show your channel name and video statistics in the app.
  • Manage comments (youtube.force-ssl) — to read comments on your videos and post replies when you enable that feature.

How data is stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows Data Protection (DPAPI).
  • Generated videos, scripts and statistics are stored only in the app's local folders on your computer.
  • The app has no server. The developer does not receive, collect or store any of your data.

How data is shared

Your data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app only sends data to Google's YouTube APIs to perform the actions you requested.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Disconnect the account in the app's Setup page, which deletes the stored tokens.
  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its folder removes all locally stored data.
